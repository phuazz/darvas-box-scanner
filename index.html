📖 Method

Box: Highest high & lowest low over N-day lookback. Represents consolidation.

▲ BUY: Day's close breaks above the rolling N-day high AND volume ≥ X× the 20-day average.

▼ SELL: Close below the box bottom (N-day low at entry). No exceptions.

Key fix in v3: The backtest uses a rolling Donchian channel approach — at each bar, the box top is the N-day highest high and box bottom is the N-day lowest low. A breakout occurs when today's close exceeds yesterday's N-day high on above-average volume. This avoids the retroactive resolution bug where pre-computed boxes couldn't identify "forming" state during the backtest.

Data: Live 5-year daily OHLCV from Yahoo Finance via CORS proxy. 42 stocks across Tech, Health, Financials, Energy, Consumer, Industrials, Utilities, Communications + SPY benchmark.
